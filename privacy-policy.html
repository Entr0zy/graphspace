GraphSpace Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

GraphSpace is made by ENTR0ZY. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

The short version

GraphSpace does not collect, store or share any of your data. There is no account and no sign-up, and the app works fully offline. Nothing you make leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Your projects

The coordinate systems you build are saved only on your device. We can't see them.

Photos

If you choose to save an exported image, GraphSpace asks to add it to your photo library. It only adds the images you export.

Files

You can open a GraphSpace project file you pick yourself. The app reads only that file.

Sharing

Images and projects you share go only where you send them, through your device's own share sheet.

No tracking

GraphSpace has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Children

GraphSpace does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a project inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
