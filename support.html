GraphSpace Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? It helps to say which device you are on. Email entrozy@protonmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two days.

Common questions

Where are my projects? Only on your device. There's no account, so nothing is backed up by us. Export a project to keep a copy elsewhere.

Saving an image doesn't work. Allow GraphSpace to add photos in your device's settings, then export again.

Can it plot my data? Not yet. GraphSpace builds the axes, scales and colours; plotting data is planned.
